# Gemini prompts for Harpo's Coat

For each prompt, upload the brothers sheet from the Groucho Clock
(`~/projects/groucho-clock/assets/src/04-brothers.jpeg`), so Harpo and the style match.
Save the results in `~/Pictures/harpos-coat/` under the names in the headings.

```

A flat, pure magenta (#FF00FF) background with no magenta anywhere on him, 4 columns by 3 rows, every frame FULL LENGTH from the top of his hat to his shoes, every frame at the SAME scale with his feet on the same line at the bottom of the frame, nothing touching or overlapping. Row 2: his "gookie" face (cheeks puffed out, eyes crossed, tongue tucked), right hand held up empty; tossing something over his shoulder with his right hand, flung back; peering down inside his open coat, puzzled; both hands deep in the coat, pulling hard, leaning back.
